## Search fixes (follow-up)
- **"2bhk" showed a 1 BHK property first:** the property offers 1BHK and 2BHK but the card only showed the first option. Cards now list ALL configurations (e.g. "1 BHK, 2 BHK"), and results are ranked so properties whose main/only configuration is the searched BHK come first. "2bhk" no longer matches "12bhk".
- **"new property" showed Luxury:** the word "property" matched every listing. Generic words (property, flat, home…) are now ignored, and section words (new, luxury, plots, hotel, commercial, international, jv, independent…) filter to that section: "new property"/"new project" → New Projects only.
- Price "N/A" now shows "Price on request".

## Video display fix
- Property page keeps the OLD layout. Videos are now tiles inside the "Photos & Videos" grid (same size as photos, with a play button). Clicking a video opens a popup and plays it there (like a photo); no redirect to YouTube/Instagram. Property details are unchanged.
